Job Copilot extension: privacy

The extension has no server and sends nothing to any server. It talks only to the Job Copilot app open in your own browser.

What it stores

After you connect it from the app (Settings, Chrome extension, Connect, then confirm the matching code), the app copies your Profile, saved answers, experience and a list of your tracked jobs (company, role, link) into the extension's storage on this device, so it can fill application forms. Resumes are fetched from the app only when you click Fill, and are not kept.

What it never does

  • It never holds or sees an API key. AI drafts are written by the app, and come back only after you approve them there.
  • It never clicks Submit, never creates an account and never enters a password.
  • It never runs on LinkedIn, Indeed or Jobright.
  • It answers work authorization, sponsorship and demographic questions only from what you saved in your Profile. Anything not saved is left blank for you.

Where it runs

On Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday pages by default. On any other site only after you click "Use on this site" in its popup, which asks Chrome for that one site. You can turn a site off again from the popup.

Removing your data

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored. Disconnecting in the app's Settings stops all syncing.
